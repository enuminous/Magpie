# Limitations and Claim Boundaries

MAGPIE is an experimental monitoring framework.

It does not prove that a claim is true merely because provenance is complete. A false source can be perfectly traceable.

It does not guarantee that contradiction retrieval is exhaustive.

It depends on the quality of claim decomposition, source access, and evidence scoring.

Its mathematical scores are operational metrics, not established laws of physics.

Thresholds must be calibrated independently and preregistered.

The framework should be simplified or rejected if recursive lineage does not outperform simpler baselines under matched conditions.
